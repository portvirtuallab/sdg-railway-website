SDG Railway Load Planner – Visual Simulation

Drag and drop ITUs into wagons. Check load balance and estimated CO₂e savings. Hover over each unit to view details.

Reference distance (km):
Balanced | Near limit | Overloaded
Reference ITU weights (typical gross):
20’ ≈ 22 t | 40’ ≈ 30 t | 45’ ≈ 32 t | P400 Trailer ≈ 34 t
Simulation basis & sources:
Typical ITU gross weights and wagon thresholds are used for educational purposes and reflect common European intermodal practices (ISO 668 context, UIC/EcoTransIT-style methodologies). CO₂e savings are estimated using average European emission factors and a fixed reference distance. This tool is intended for training and visual simulation only and does not represent certified operational limits.
