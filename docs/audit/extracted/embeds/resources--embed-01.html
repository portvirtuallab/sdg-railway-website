Axle Load
The total weight distributed over each axle of a railway vehicle. It determines track capacity and safety limits.
SDG 9 – Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure
Bi-Mode Locomotive
A locomotive capable of operating both under electric power (from overhead lines) and diesel engines. It enables flexibility on mixed routes and reduces emissions.
SDG 7 – Affordable and Clean Energy
CO₂e (Carbon Dioxide Equivalent)
A unit used to compare the emissions of different greenhouse gases based on their global warming potential.
SDG 13 – Climate Action
Combined Transport
A logistics system where goods are moved using more than one mode of transport (e.g., rail and road) with minimal handling.
SDG 11 – Sustainable Cities and Communities
Corridor
A strategic route connecting major terminals or regions, facilitating efficient freight flows across countries or continents.
Dangerous Goods (DG)
Substances or materials that pose risks to health, safety, property, or the environment. Transported under international regulations such as ADR/RID.
Digitalization
The integration of digital tools and systems (e.g., ERPs, simulators, smart sensors) to optimize logistics and operational efficiency.
SDG 9 – Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure
Energy Efficiency
The use of less energy to perform the same transport operations, reducing environmental impact.
SDG 7 – Affordable and Clean Energy
Eco-Performance
The balance between transport productivity and its environmental footprint. Often measured by emissions per ton-kilometer.
Freight Train
A train designed for carrying goods, containers, or semi-trailers instead of passengers. Essential for sustainable supply chains.
SDG 12 – Responsible Consumption and Production
Gate In / Gate Out
The operational process at terminals where loading units (containers or trailers) enter (Gate In) or exit (Gate Out) controlled facilities for transport or delivery.
Green Corridor
A freight route designed to prioritize low-emission and sustainable transport methods.
SDG 13 – Climate Action
Intermodal Transport
The movement of goods using two or more transport modes within a single transport chain, without handling the goods themselves when changing modes.
SDG 9 – Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure
ITU (Intermodal Transport Unit)
A standardized unit (e.g., container, swap body, or trailer) designed to be used in intermodal transport chains.
Load Planner Tool
A digital application that simulates wagon loading, checking balance, axle loads, and CO₂ impact before train departure.
Modal Shift
The transition of freight from road to more sustainable modes like rail or sea, reducing congestion and emissions.

Railway Gauge
The distance between the inner sides of the two parallel rails. Standard gauge in Europe is 1,435 mm.
Railway Corridor
A network section used for national or international freight operations, often forming part of Trans-European Transport Networks (TEN-T).
SDG 9 – Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure
RID (Regulations Concerning the International Carriage of Dangerous Goods by Rail)
The European regulation governing the safe transport of dangerous goods by rail. It establishes classification, packaging, labeling, and documentation requirements to ensure safety in international freight operations.

Sustainability
The commitment to balancing economic growth, environmental care, and social inclusion through innovative logistics.
Terminal
A facility where goods are transferred between transport modes (road, rail, sea). Equipped for storage, handling, and customs procedures.
Track Gauge
The standardized width between rails determining the type of train that can operate on a given line.
